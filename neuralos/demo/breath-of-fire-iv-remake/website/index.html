An old legend, told again

A fan-made turn-based RPG about dragons, debts and the people caught between them.

Join the playtest

Features

  • Elemental combat. Exploit weaknesses to fire, ice, wind and earth, and chain attacks into combos.
  • A living economy. Merchants travel between towns, restock after you rest, and remember a good haggle.
  • Swap heroes mid-journey. Keep four heroes in your party and rotate the rest from the reserve.
  • Hand-drawn towns. Every shop, harbor and save point is painted by hand.

The story

When a merchant caravan vanishes on the desert road, a young courier named Kael sets out to find it. What he finds instead is a Dragon Tear, still warm, and a war that both empires would rather forget.
