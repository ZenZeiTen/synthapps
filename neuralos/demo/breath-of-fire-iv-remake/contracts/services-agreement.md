# Services Agreement

This Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 1 July 2026 between
**Remake Demo Team**, an unincorporated volunteer group (the "Client"), and
**Pixel Harbor Studio**, a sole proprietorship (the "Contractor"). The Client
and the Contractor are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

This is a fictional agreement written for demonstration purposes.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Services" means the pixel-art production described in Schedule A.

1.2 "Deliverables" means the sprite sheets, tile sets and source files the
Contractor produces under this Agreement.

1.3 "Acceptance" means the Client's written confirmation that a Deliverable
meets the specification in Schedule A.

1.4 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday
in Jakarta, Indonesia.

## 2. Services

2.1 The Contractor shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care
and deliver each Deliverable by the date in Schedule A.

2.2 The Client shall provide reference material and feedback within five (5)
Business Days of each request.

## 3. Acceptance

3.1 The Client shall review each Deliverable within ten (10) Business Days of
delivery and either confirm Acceptance or list the defects in writing.

3.2 If the Client does neither within that period, the Deliverable is deemed
accepted.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 The Client shall pay the Contractor a fixed fee of IDR 45,000,000 (forty-five
million rupiah) for the Services.

4.2 The fee is payable in three instalments: thirty percent (30%) on signature,
forty percent (40%) on Acceptance of the character sprites, and thirty percent
(30%) on Acceptance of the final Deliverable.

4.3 The Contractor shall issue an invoice for each instalment. The Client shall
pay each invoice within fourteen (14) days of receipt by bank transfer.

4.4 Late payments bear interest at one percent (1%) per month from the due date
until paid.

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 On full payment, the Contractor assigns to the Client all copyright in the
Deliverables.

5.2 The Contractor may show the Deliverables in its portfolio after the game's
public release.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential any non-public information received
from the other Party and use it only to perform this Agreement.

## 7. Term and Termination

7.1 This Agreement starts on the date above and ends on Acceptance of the final
Deliverable.

7.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by thirty (30) days' written
notice. The Client shall pay for Services performed up to the termination date.

## 8. Governing Law

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Indonesia.
Disputes shall first be settled by negotiation in good faith.
