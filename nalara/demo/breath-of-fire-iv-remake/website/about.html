About the team

Who we are

We are six volunteers in three time zones: two programmers, two artists, a composer and a writer. We build the game in the open, one small release at a time.

How we work

Design decisions are written down before code is written. Every module has tests, and every change is reviewed by someone who did not write it.

Translations

The game and this website will be available in English and Indonesian. Want to help translate? Read our glossary and get in touch.
